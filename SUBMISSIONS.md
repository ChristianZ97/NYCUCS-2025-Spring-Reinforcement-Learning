## Identity notice

The exact documents intentionally retain the information visible at submission
time. The homework reports include an author name, student identifier, school
email address, and—in screenshots—historical local filesystem paths. Team
documents identify all three contributors, their affiliations and contact
details; the final report also records the submitted contribution split. The
PowerPoint package retains its original creator and last-editor metadata.

These files are included with publication approval as historical authored
work, not as reusable personal data. Course handouts and guidelines, source
ZIPs, the final code ZIP, and the poster-feedback workbook remain excluded.
